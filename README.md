# Overview
This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard built in Power BI to analyze business performance from 2014–2017 using the Superstore dataset.

The dashboard enables users to monitor key business metrics, identify sales trends, evaluate product performance, analyze customer behavior, and compare regional performance through interactive visualizations.

# Business Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance
Identify top-performing products
Compare regional sales contribution
Evaluate customer segments
Monitor revenue and profit margin
Track monthly and quarterly sales trends
Analyze return orders
Provide interactive filtering for business users

# Business Insights
1. West region generated the highest sales contribution.
2. Consumer segment accounted for the largest share of sales.
3. Phones and Chairs were among the highest-selling products.
4. Sales peaked during Quarter 4 and year-end months.
4. Return orders represented a very small percentage of total orders.
5. Profit margin remained approximately 12%.
